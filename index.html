How it works

Small steps, big smiles

Clocklings guides kids from their very first “o'clock” all the way to reading exact minutes.

  1. Learn

    Friendly lessons show what the hands do, with animated demos and a narrator who reads everything aloud.

  2. Practise

    Read the clock, then set it yourself. A gentle hint helps after a mistake, so nobody gets stuck.

  3. Earn stars

    Every level can earn up to three stars. Play again any time to beat your best.

  4. Dress up

    Stars unlock hats, crowns, scarves and more. Over 140 outfit pieces wait in the wardrobe.

The learning path

  1. 1

    Whole hours

    The long hand points straight up to 12. Read the short hand and you know the hour.

  2. 2

    Half hours

    The long hand has gone halfway around to 6. The short hand sits between two numbers.

  3. 3

    Quarter hours

    Quarter past at 3, quarter to at 9. The clock face splits into four easy slices.

  4. 4

    Five minutes

    Every number is worth five minutes. Count in fives as the long hand travels.

  5. 5

    Exact minutes

    The little marks are single minutes. Count to the last five, then add the rest.

  6. 6

    The 24-hour day

    The short hand goes around twice a day. 15:30 is the same as half past three in the afternoon.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Who is Clocklings for?

    Children who are starting to learn the analog clock. Lessons begin with what the two hands do, so no earlier knowledge is needed.

    Which devices does it run on?

    Android phones and tablets running Android 8.0 or newer. It also adapts to foldables and larger screens.

    Is there an iPhone or iPad version?

    Not yet. Clocklings launches on Android first.

    Does the free version expire?

    No. The free part stays free for as long as you like. Upgrade only if your child wants more.

    Does my child need to read?

    Not really. Instructions, hints and times can be read aloud, and the companion shows how things work.

    Can we use the 24-hour clock?

    Yes. A setting switches between 12-hour and 24-hour time, and a final lesson explains how the hours of the whole day work.
